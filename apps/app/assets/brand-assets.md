# Brand assets

`claude-symbol.svg` is the Claude symbol from the official https://claude.com header, retrieved 2026-09-24. Its original path and brand color (#D97757) are retained. Used to identify sessions opened in Claude.

`codex-logo.png` is the original Codex GA logo bundled in the installed official ChatGPT desktop app (`webview/assets/codex-app-ga-logo-3e5209898ca3.png`), copied 2026-09-24. The original blue/purple artwork and transparency are retained.

`linear-light.svg` and `linear-dark.svg` are the unmodified logomarks from https://static.linear.app/design-assets/Linear-Brand-Assets.zip?v=3, linked by https://linear.app/brand, retrieved 2026-09-24.
