# Mondash icon

Generated with the built-in image generator, then resized to an opaque 1024 × 1024 PNG using macOS `sips`. Final asset: `mobile/assets/mondash-icon.png`. Square, full bleed artwork; the operating system supplies the icon mask.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create the final production iOS app icon artwork for Mondash, a personal Monday work dashboard connecting issues, reviews and discussions. Square 1024 x 1024 image. A beautifully simple, bold geometric M monogram constructed from three flowing dashboard-like strokes, with one small horizontal dash integrated into the mark. Polished contemporary Apple-platform app aesthetic, exceptionally clear at tiny sizes, distinctive balanced geometry. Center the symbol at roughly 62 percent of the canvas width with generous breathing room. Solid plain deep midnight navy background, full bleed right up to all four square edges. Symbol in crisp warm white with one restrained vivid sky-blue accent. Flat graphic design with clean edges. No text or app name, no rounded-square tile, no outer border, no frame, no corner rounding, no drop shadow, no glass or bevel, no background texture or gradient, no mockup or phone. The delivered image itself is the square app artwork; iOS will apply its own icon mask.
